Everything a weekly publishing routine needs — and nothing it doesn't.

DruBuzz is a planner, not another inbox. It does three things properly.

Per-platform previews

See the real thing before it ships — X's 280-character bar, LinkedIn's headline line, Instagram's square crop. The over-limit warning turns red so nothing gets truncated in public.

Week-at-a-glance calendar

Every channel on one grid, colour-coded by status. Drag a post to a new slot, spot the empty Saturday, and fill next week before it arrives.

Review workflow built in

Editors draft, reviewers approve. A post moves Draft → In review → Scheduled with a clear owner at every step, so nothing publishes that nobody signed off.
